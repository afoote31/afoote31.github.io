In the process of applying to graduate school in computer science, I stumbled on to some resources that I found useful for framing my thinking. These aren't my own opinions but I generally agree with their thinking.

Professor [Shriram Krishnamurthi](https://cs.brown.edu/~sk/) at Brown -- [writing letters of rec](https://cs.brown.edu/~sk/Memos/Grad-School-Recos/)

Professor [Mor Harchol-Balter](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~harchol/) at CMU -- [general guidance for the application process](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~harchol/gradschooltalk.pdf)

Professor [Daniela Witten](https://www.danielawitten.com/) at UW -- [opinions on various non-graduate school topics](https://www.danielawitten.com/written-by-witten)

Professor [John Baez](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/README.html) at UC Riverside -- [advice for the young scientist](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/advice.html) and [teaching](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/teaching.html)

Professor [J. Michael Steele](http://www-stat.wharton.upenn.edu/~steele/) at Penn -- [general guidance](http://www-stat.wharton.upenn.edu/~steele/Rants/AdviceGS.html), one of [many rants](http://www-stat.wharton.upenn.edu/~steele/Rants.htm)

Professor [Terence Tao](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/) at UCLA -- [career advice](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/career-advice/) with *lots* of other links

Dr. [Jean Yang](https://www.jeanyang.com/) -- [a series of posts about applying to graduate school](https://jxyzabc.blogspot.com/2008/08/cs-grad-school-part-1-deciding-to-apply.html) along with a variety of [other interesting posts](https://jxyzabc.blogspot.com/)
